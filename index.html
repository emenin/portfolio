AI + Design Systems

A design system built only for people is easy for AI to get wrong.

Hover the text to see what AI reads as ambiguous or explicit

Beautiful storytelling, visual examples, and decisions left between the lines all leave space for guesses. And guesses make messes.

We don’t have all the answers yet. But one thing is clear: we have to be more intentional. Straightforward rules, README files, component guidance in code, design contracts, you name it.

The format matters less than the intent: we can’t ignore how machines read the system and just hope pray they make the right decisions.

rule
Make design system decisions explicit.
why
AI can’t read between the lines. When a decision is unwritten or unclear, it guesses.
how
There’s no single format. Pick one or more (rules, READMEs, guidance in code, design contracts) and keep each one straightforward.

That’s where my work is now: making design system decisions easier for people and AI to find, apply, and trust.

In practice: turning our documentation checklist into a skill and my first Cursor command.

Beliefs

👻

Invisible is good.

Good design system work often goes unnoticed: fewer repeated questions, clearer decisions, and less time fighting the system.

🪄

AI shouldn’t replace us.

AI composes validly. A human composes well. Valid and good are not the same thing: AI can pick the right components, break no rules, and still miss the point.

📓

More documentation ≠ clarity.

Cover the basics. Then add depth where the system is already showing you the need: repeated questions, recurring mistakes, and places where people or AI get stuck over and over.

🤖

AI won’t fix a messy system.

Fix the foundations first. If components, rules, examples, docs, Figma, and code don’t agree, AI will only make the mess louder.
